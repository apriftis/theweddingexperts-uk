Before you sign up

Is it free to list?

Yes. A free listing includes your business name, description, phone and monthly stats. Paid tiers add more photos, reviews and wider reach, and they stay optional. UK prices will be published at launch.

How do couples reach me?

In three ways. They can call you straight from your profile. They can send you a request from your profile, with their date, guests and a message. Or they post a brief with the services they need, and once our team approves it, up to five suppliers per category in that area can unlock their details and get in touch.

Do I send quotes through the app?

No. You unlock a brief and message the couple. Couples can also send you a Quote Request from your profile, with their date, guests, budget and a message.

What does a founding listing get me?

We build your profile before launch, at no cost, and you keep priority placement in your county through the first season.
