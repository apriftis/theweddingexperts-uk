Questions.

What does a listing cost?

Nothing. A free listing includes your business name, description and phone number. Paid plans add a gallery, reviews, messages and a starting price, and are optional. UK prices will be confirmed before launch.

How do couples reach me?

In three ways. They can call you straight from your profile. They can send you a request from your profile, with their date, guests and a message. Or they post a brief with the services they need, and once our team approves it, up to five suppliers per category in that area can unlock their details and get in touch.

Why sign up before launch?

We build founding profiles at no cost before couples arrive, so you are already listed on day one. Founding suppliers also keep priority placement in their county through the first season.
