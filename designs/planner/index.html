Questions suppliers ask.

What does a founding listing cost?

Nothing. We build your profile before launch at no cost. A free listing stays free and covers your name, description, phone and monthly stats. Paid tiers add more, stay optional, and UK prices will be set at launch.

Do I send quotes through the app?

No, there is no quote builder. When a brief fits, you unlock the couple's details and message them. Couples can also send a quote request from your profile with their date, guests and budget.

How do couples reach me?

In three ways. They can call you straight from your profile. They can send you a request from your profile, with their date, guests and a message. Or they post a brief with the services they need, and once our team approves it, up to five suppliers per category in that area can unlock their details and get in touch.

When will couples be able to find me?

When we open to couples in the UK. We will be in touch well before then, and founding suppliers keep priority placement in their county through the first season.
