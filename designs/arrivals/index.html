Before you sign up.

Does a listing cost anything?

No. A listing is free, and founding profiles are built at no cost. Paid plans are optional, and UK prices are set at launch.

How do couples reach me?

In three ways. They can call you straight from your profile. They can send you a request from your profile, with their date, guests and a message. Or they post a brief with the services they need, and once our team approves it, up to five suppliers per category in that area can unlock their details and get in touch.

Why only five suppliers per category?

So a brief is not sent to every supplier in the county. Up to five suppliers in each category can unlock a couple's name, email and phone.

Do I send quotes through the app?

No. You unlock a brief and message the couple directly. Couples can also send you a quote request from your profile.
