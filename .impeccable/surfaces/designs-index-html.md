## Direction contract

### A. Arrivals (assigned, seed 63b95059, candidate 7)

THESIS: Couples' briefs arrive like trains on a station departures board, by place and date, with seats running out. Refuses the soft-focus wedding-photo hero.
OWN-WORLD: Board navy-black ground, split-flap cells in warm amber with a hinge line, platform white type, one signal green for "seats open". Condensed display grotesk plus a monospace for flap cells only. Square corners.
STORY: Briefs land in your area; only five suppliers per category get them; founding suppliers are on the board first; list now.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Left 5 cols headline and CTA; right 7 cols a live flap board of four UK briefs flipping in; logo top left.
