The Old Deanery is a Jacobean house built in 1625, standing beside Ripon Cathedral in the heart of the city. On your wedding day the house, its grounds and its gardens are yours alone, from 11am until 11am the next morning.

Between 60 and 80 guests can sit down for the wedding breakfast, and eleven bedrooms across the house mean family and friends can stay the night. Dogs are welcome too.

The team looks after the day from planning to the last dance: drinks and canapés after the ceremony, a three-course wedding breakfast, evening food and a private bar until midnight, with a duty manager as your master of ceremonies.

Frequently asked questions

How many guests can you seat?

Between 60 and 80 seated guests for the daytime wedding breakfast.

Do we have the venue to ourselves?

Yes. You have exclusive use of the house, grounds and gardens from 11am on your wedding day until 11am the next morning.

Can we hold our ceremony at The Old Deanery?

Yes. The Old Deanery is on the General Register Office list of venues approved for civil ceremonies.

What does the wedding package include?

Reception drinks and canapés, a three-course wedding breakfast with half a bottle of wine per guest, evening grazing food, a private bar until midnight, a duty manager as master of ceremonies, and a cake knife, cake stand and table linen.

Can guests stay overnight?

Yes. There are 11 bedrooms on the first to third floors. There is no lift.

Are dogs welcome?

Yes, dogs are welcome in the bedrooms and public areas.
