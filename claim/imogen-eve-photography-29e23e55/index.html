Imogen Eve Photography is a candid wedding photography business run by Imogen, based in Surrey and London and covering the UK, Europe and destinations further afield. Her style blends documentary storytelling with a fashion-led, editorial eye, and she is clear that couples do not need to know how to pose.

Imogen has spent six years photographing weddings and has covered more than 300 celebrations, from London town halls and Surrey countryside days to Lake Como, Tuscany, Paris, Marrakech and the Greek islands. She keeps a discreet presence through the day and takes a short, intentional window for portraits, so couples stay with their guests. She takes on a limited number of weddings each year.

Couples receive a curated preview within 24 hours and their full gallery within four weeks. The 'I do' coverage for town hall weddings and elopements gives up to two hours of photography and starts from £1,200, and there are two further core packages as well as bespoke options. Imogen has been named a Top 50 UK Wedding Photographer in 2024 and 2025.

Frequently asked questions

What is your photography style?

Candid and editorial. Imogen blends documentary storytelling with a fashion-led eye, and says couples do not need to know how to pose.

How much does wedding photography cost?

The 'I do' package, for town hall weddings and elopements, covers up to two hours and starts from £1,200. There are three core packages in all, and a bespoke package can be created to suit your day.

How soon will we see our photos?

A curated preview arrives within 24 hours, and the full, individually edited gallery is delivered within four weeks, with an online gallery with downloads for friends and family.

Where do you work?

Imogen is based in Surrey and London and regularly photographs weddings across the UK and Europe, including Italy, France, Morocco, Iceland, the Scottish Highlands and the Greek Islands. She is happy to travel further.

How many weddings do you take on?

A limited number each year, which Imogen says lets her be fully present at every one.

How do we book?

Fill in the enquiry form, then Imogen sends her availability and full brochure and can book a call if you prefer. A deposit and contract confirm the booking.
