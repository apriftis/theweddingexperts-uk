The BarnYard is a family-run wedding venue and restaurant in Upchurch, between Rainham and Sittingbourne in Kent. It sits in 150 acres of apple and pear orchards, was established in 1984 and was once known as Gore Farm. Couples can choose between The Arboretum, a permanent glass pavilion, and The Apple Barn, an oak-beamed barn strung with fairy lights.

The Arboretum holds up to 200 guests by day and 100 more in the evening, and The Apple Barn holds up to 180 across the evening. There are five ceremony settings, including the Lavender Walk gazebo, the Orchard Garden gazebo and the Courtyard, and the venue is licensed for civil ceremonies. Packages run from an Intimate wedding for up to 30 guests to a Signature package with exclusive use of the whole estate, and a dry hire option for The Arboretum is also quoted by date.

The BarnYard is run by Joanne and David McInnes. Food is cooked on site and locally sourced, full-day packages include food, drink and a dedicated coordinator, and there are bridal dressing suites for getting ready.

Frequently asked questions

How many guests can you host?

The Arboretum holds up to 200 guests in the daytime and 100 more in the evening. The Apple Barn takes up to 85 for a ceremony and 180 across the evening, and the Intimate package is for up to 30 guests.

What wedding packages do you offer?

There are four. Traditional is a full day in the Arboretum from £6,750 all-inclusive. Twilight is an evening ceremony and party in the Apple Barn from £3,450 venue hire. Intimate is for up to 30 guests from £5,000 all-inclusive. Signature gives exclusive use of the whole estate and is priced on request.

Can we hold the ceremony at The BarnYard?

Yes. The BarnYard is licensed for civil ceremonies, with a registrar from the Kent Registrars booked through the team. There are five settings, including the Lavender Walk gazebo, the Orchard Garden gazebo, the Courtyard, the Arboretum and the Apple Barn.

Is food and drink included?

Traditional, Intimate and Signature packages are all-inclusive, covering the food and drink for the day. With Twilight, the venue hire is priced separately and you choose from buffet menus priced per head.

Can we get ready on site?

Yes. The packages include dressing suites for the morning, and the Signature package includes two luxury bridal dressing suites.

Can we visit before booking?

Yes. You can book a private tour or a viewing through the website, and there are wedding open days. The office is open Wednesday to Sunday, 9am to 4pm.
