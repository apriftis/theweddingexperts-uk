Beatrice Espinosa Bridal is a bespoke bridal atelier in Bentley, Hampshire, on the Surrey border between Farnham and Alton. Beatrice founded it in 2015 after ten years working freelance in theatre set and costume design, and designs and makes every dress by hand in her studio.

Couples can commission a bespoke wedding dress, with hand-drawn designs, a calico toile, fittings and a couture finish, or choose from her Silk Bridal Collection, made in your size. She also makes bridal separates such as lace jackets, skirts and capes, and offers bridal alterations, from resizing to restyling vintage dresses.

Appointments are by arrangement at the studio, with video calls available for the first conversation. Beatrice also offers a getting-ready service on the wedding morning, and serves the wider area of Guildford, Winchester, Southampton and London.

Frequently asked questions

How long should we allow to order a bespoke dress?

Allow a minimum of 6 to 9 months before the wedding date.

How much does a bespoke dress cost?

Couture dresses start at £2,000 plus fabric. Quotes include the design and material sourcing, the calico toile, time for fittings and a couture finish, and a deposit is taken when you book.

Do you offer a getting-ready service on the day?

Yes. For £150 within a 10 mile radius, Beatrice steams the dress, dresses the bride and arranges the dress for photos. Further afield is quoted on request.

Can we start with a video call?

Yes. The first conversation about styles, fabrics and prices can be a video call, though all other appointments and fittings take place at the studio.

Can we bring guests to appointments?

Yes, generally up to two. Ask ahead if you would like to bring more, and allow 1 to 1.5 hours if the bride and bridesmaids are being fitted together.

Do you do wedding dress alterations?

Yes. Alterations include complete resizing, restyling, vintage dress refurbishment, customising an existing dress, and shortening or taking in, as well as very small adjustments.
