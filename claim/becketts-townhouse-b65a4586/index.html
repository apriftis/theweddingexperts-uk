Becketts Townhouse is a boutique hotel, cocktail bar and private venue in a restored Georgian townhouse on Bellevue Terrace in Southsea, Portsmouth. It is run by owners Jodie Coles and Jason Parker, and has been part of Portsmouth for over 30 years.

For weddings it hosts intimate weddings, wedding receptions, evening celebrations, wedding breakfasts and post-ceremony or registry office celebrations for 30 to 120 guests. There is no venue hire fee: couples agree a minimum bar spend instead, and exclusive hire covers the Main Bar, the Snug and the Vault party room, seven days a week.

Extras include a hot fork buffet or grazing buffets, a Prosecco Wall, a resident DJ, a photobooth, a Sugar Bar and venue decor. Eight boutique bedrooms upstairs mean family and friends can stay on after the celebrations.

Frequently asked questions

Do you charge a venue hire fee?

No. Becketts works on a minimum bar spend instead of a room hire fee. The spend varies with the day of the week and the wedding package, and the team gives a personalised quotation.

How many guests can you host?

Exclusive full venue hire is for 30 to 120 guests, in the Main Bar, the Snug and the Vault party room, seven days a week.

What catering do you offer?

A hot fork buffet with two courses at £39.95 per person suits a sit-down wedding breakfast for up to 45 guests, and grazing buffets start at £12.95 per person for 50 to 120 guests.

Can our guests stay overnight?

Yes. Becketts has eight boutique bedrooms on site, and exclusive guest rates are available for wedding parties.

Can we bring our own cake, decorations or DJ?

Yes to all three. You can bring your own cake (the team provides a cake knife), personalise the space with decorations, and arrange your own DJ or entertainment, subject to venue guidelines. A resident DJ is also available.

Is parking available?

There is on-street parking near Becketts and several public car parks within easy walking distance. Portsmouth and Southsea station is a short walk away, and there is a bus stop right outside.
