David Baker Disco Experience is a wedding and event DJ service based in Wickham Bishops, Essex, run by David. The site describes award-winning wedding DJ services, from ceremony music and drinks reception playlists to a lively evening, using high-quality sound and lighting.

David works with each couple on a personalised playlist, with must-play and do-not-play lists, and takes requests on the night. Customer reviews on the site mention pre-wedding meetings to go through playlists and timings, and weddings in barns, marquees and courtyards.

As well as weddings, David hosts outdoor sporting, charity and community events, including Race for Life, Ultra X events across the UK and charity events for Samaritans and Havens Hospices. He covers Essex and the surrounding areas.

Frequently asked questions

What kind of music do you play?

A wide range of genres, from classic wedding anthems to modern hits. Couples are encouraged to share their must-play and do-not-play lists to build a personalised soundtrack.

Do you offer lighting and effects?

Yes. Lighting ranges from subtle mood lighting to dance floor effects, and packages can be tailored to your requirements.

How do you handle guest requests?

David is happy to take requests on the night, and has a process for handling them so the flow of the evening is not interrupted.

What equipment do you use?

A high-quality sound system, microphones for speeches and professional DJ equipment, with backup equipment ready.

Can you play at outdoor weddings?

Yes. The site says the setup works for garden venues and marquee receptions, with contingencies for weather.

Can you play music for the ceremony and drinks reception too?

Yes. Services run from ceremony music and drinks reception playlists through to the evening party.
