Warren Lodge Hotel is a riverside hotel in Church Square, Shepperton, on the Thames, with 50 bedrooms and its own restaurant. It hosts wedding receptions in the Garden Room and the Coach House, with a courtyard and a River View Terrace for drinks and photographs.

The Garden Room seats up to 60 for the wedding breakfast, with space for another 60 in the evening, and the Coach House is a private dining room for up to 20. Three all-inclusive packages start at 40 guests and include a dedicated wedding coordinator, a toastmaster, table linen, a private function bar and a dance floor. The couple get a complimentary Club bedroom overlooking the Thames, with a full English breakfast.

The hotel does not have a ceremony licence. St Nicholas' Church is right outside, and Weybridge Registry is a short drive away, so couples hold the ceremony there and celebrate at Warren Lodge. There is also a Twilight wedding for evening-only celebrations, and guests can stay at preferential bedroom rates.

Frequently asked questions

How many guests can you host?

The Garden Room seats up to 60 for the wedding breakfast, with space for another 60 in the evening. The all-inclusive packages cater for up to 80 evening guests. The Coach House is a private dining room for up to 20.

Can we hold our ceremony at Warren Lodge?

No, the hotel does not have a wedding ceremony licence. St Nicholas' Church is right outside the hotel for religious ceremonies, and Weybridge Registry is a short drive away. Couples arrange the Registrar themselves.

What do the wedding packages include?

There are three all-inclusive packages for a minimum of 40 guests. All include table linen, cake presentation tables, crockery and cutlery, a professional toastmaster, a private function bar, a dance floor and a personal wedding coordinator. The 2027 brochure prices start at £3,850 for 40 guests.

Is there an evening-only option?

Yes. The Twilight wedding package covers room hire, a DJ, a glass of Prosecco on arrival, two canapés, a six-piece finger buffet, chair covers and sashes and centrepieces. It is £68 per person for a minimum of 50 guests, with up to 100 guests for the reception.

Can we stay the night?

Yes. The couple get a complimentary Club bedroom overlooking the Thames with a full English breakfast, and guests attending the reception get special bedroom rates. The hotel has 50 bedrooms.

Can we bring our own flowers and entertainment?

Yes, flowers and entertainment are up to you. Food and drink are provided by the hotel as part of the package, and your private function bar stays open until midnight.
