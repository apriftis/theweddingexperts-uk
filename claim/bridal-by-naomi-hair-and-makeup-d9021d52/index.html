Bridal By Naomi is a freelance makeup artist and hair stylist for weddings and elopements, based in Bristol and working across the South West. Naomi qualified as a makeup artist almost 15 years ago and has worked on the counter for brands including Estee Lauder, Smashbox, MAC and Bobbi Brown.

Her style is naturally beautiful, effortless makeup and hair that still looks like you. Couples start with a trial, where you work through ideas together, and on the day Naomi comes to wherever you are getting ready. She does makeup and hair for the bride, mums, sisters and bridesmaids, and works with trusted assistants for larger bridal parties.

Naomi is the recommended makeup and hair supplier for the Ceremonies Team at Bristol Register Office, and for Hotel Du Vin in Bristol, Pennard House in Somerset and The Dragons Gate in Hereford. She uses cruelty-free products with vegan options, and can offer a fully vegan service.

Frequently asked questions

How much does bridal makeup and hair cost?

Bridal packages start from £350 and can be tailored across makeup, hair or both, depending on what you need. Naomi gives each couple a quote once she has the details of the day.

Do you offer a trial?

Yes. You meet beforehand for a trial to get to know each other and try out ideas before the wedding. If you are travelling to the UK, Naomi offers a virtual trial before you arrive.

Can you do makeup and hair for the bridal party?

Yes. Naomi does makeup and hair for the bride, mums, sisters and bridesmaids, and works with a team of trusted assistants for larger bridal parties.

Where do you travel to?

Naomi travels wherever you are getting ready across the South West, from Bristol to Wales, Cheltenham and Gloucester, the Mendips, the Cotswolds, Devon and Cornwall, and to London on request.

Do you offer vegan or cruelty-free products?

Naomi is moving her kit to be entirely cruelty-free, with plenty of vegan and organic options, and can provide a fully vegan service for both makeup and hair.

Do you work with elopements?

Yes. Naomi offers makeup and hair for elopements, including a makeup design appointment for couples marrying overseas, with pointers on recreating the look and a shopping list of what to pack.
