Toni Todd Bridal is a bridal hair and makeup artist based in East Preston, near Littlehampton and Arundel, in West Sussex. Toni creates soft, natural-looking bridal hair and makeup, from elegant updos and veil styling to airbrush makeup, for the bride, bridesmaids, mums and guests.

Couples start with an enquiry and a short bridal call, then book a preview session at Toni's boutique studio before the wedding. Toni sends a bespoke timeline for the morning and arrives early on the day. There are three packages, the Bridal Edit for intimate and weekday weddings, the Signature and the Opulent, and a small team of artists can look after larger bridal parties.

Toni started her wedding business in 2012, after a salon career and a degree in hair and makeup for film, TV and theatre, and opened her boutique in 2018. Since early 2024 she has focused on weddings. She is a recommended supplier at venues including Farbridge, Upwaltham, Cissbury, Amberley Castle, Selden Barns and Arundel Town Hall, and travels across West and East Sussex, Hampshire, Surrey and Kent.

Frequently asked questions

How far ahead should we book?

Toni recommends booking as soon as your wedding date is set, ideally 12 to 18 months ahead. You can check availability with the enquiry form, and she replies within 24 hours.

Do you offer a trial?

Yes. Toni recommends a bridal preview before the wedding, held at her boutique studio. Sessions usually take around 2 to 3 hours. Bring inspiration photos, your veil or hair accessories, and wear a white or light top.

Do you come to us on the wedding morning?

Yes. Toni works on site at your home, hotel or venue. If your venue only opens its getting-ready room late in the morning, she can arrange a split prep, starting at your home, hotel or Airbnb and moving to the venue when it opens.

Which areas do you cover, and is travel included?

Mainly West Sussex and East Sussex, plus nearby Hampshire, Surrey and Kent. Travel within the first 15 miles is included, with extra charges for longer journeys, and she will go further afield with mileage and accommodation added.

Can you look after the whole bridal party?

Yes. Toni can add hair and makeup for bridesmaids, mums and guests to any package, and a small team of experienced artists can accommodate bridal parties of all sizes.

What makeup do you use?

Toni uses professional-grade, vegan friendly and cruelty free brands, including Airbase Airbrush Makeup, Hourglass, Tarte, Kat Von D, Illamasqua and Kryolan.
