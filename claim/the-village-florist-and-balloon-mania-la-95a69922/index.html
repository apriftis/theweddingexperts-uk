The Village Florist is a family-run florist on North Road in Lancing, West Sussex, with over 60 years of combined floristry experience. For weddings it makes bridal bouquets and buttonholes, corsages, ceremony flowers and reception decorations, as little or as much as a couple needs.

There is no minimum spend on wedding flowers, and the service suits small, intimate celebrations as well as larger ceremonies. Couples can book a free, no-obligation consultation to talk through ideas, flowers and budget, by appointment.

The team creates wedding flowers for venues across Sussex, including Cissbury Barns, Brookfield Barn, Farbridge Barn, Highley Manor, Ravenswood, Field Place, Upwaltham Barns and Southend Barns, and is a recommended florist for Brookfield Barn, Cissbury Barns and Selden Barns. The business is run by owners Leanne and Debbie, who are qualified florists, with a team of six in all.

Frequently asked questions

What wedding flowers do you provide?

Bridal bouquets and buttonholes, corsages, ceremony flowers and reception decorations. You can have as much or as little as you need, from a few finishing touches to flowers throughout the day.

Is there a minimum spend?

No. There is no minimum spend for wedding flowers, and the team works with small, intimate celebrations as well as larger ones.

Do you offer a consultation?

Yes, a free, no-obligation consultation to talk through your ideas, flowers and budget. The team asks you to book an appointment in advance so they can give you their full attention.

Which venues do you work at?

Cissbury Barns, Brookfield Barn, Farbridge Barn, Highley Manor, Ravenswood, Field Place, Upwaltham Barns, Southend Barns and many more. The Village Florist is a recommended florist for Brookfield Barn, Cissbury Barns and Selden Barns.

Where are you based, and how far do you cover?

The shop is on North Road in Lancing, open Monday to Saturday. The business delivers across a wide area from Chichester to Peacehaven along the coast and inland to Crawley.

Who will be making our flowers?

The shop is run by owners Leanne and Debbie, both qualified florists, alongside a team of four other florists.
