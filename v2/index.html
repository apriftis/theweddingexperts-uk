Before you sign up

Questions professionals ask.

Does it cost anything?

Registering and listing your business is free. Paid plans add a larger gallery, reviews, chat and higher placement. They are optional, and UK prices will be shared before launch.

Do I have to quote inside the app?

No. You unlock a lead and message the couple, or take their call. How you price and quote stays your own.

Who builds my profile?

We set it up from the details you send us. After that you update photos, prices and services yourself, whenever you like.

I don't have a website. Can I still join?

Yes. A website or social page is optional. Your profile can be the place couples see your work.
